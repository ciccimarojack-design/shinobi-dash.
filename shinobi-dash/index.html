How to play
  • Tap anywhere to jump. Tap again in the air to flip into a double jump.
  • Swipe anywhere to dash: you slash your katana and blast forward. One dash per jump.
  • A red banner means the next gap is too wide to jump. Swipe to dash!
  • Jump barrels from 150m, stone lanterns from 800m, and watch for crows from 500m. Dash through them to slice them for +10.
  • Coins you grab are saved for the shop.
  • Power-ups: Magnet pulls in coins, ×2 doubles coins, Shield saves you from one crash or fall.
  • Every 600m the world changes: sunset, moonlit night, then snowy peaks.
Keyboard: Space or ↑ to jump, D or → to dash, P to pause
